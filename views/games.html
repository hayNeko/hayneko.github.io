Combo colour

The number in the HUD shows the combo. It breaks after 4 placements in a row without a clear — each miss drains the colour one step:

  • No combo, or the streak was broken
  • Combo just triggered
  • 1st miss
  • 2nd miss
  • 3rd miss — one more and the combo is gone

How a bounce becomes damage

Both balls always bounce to exactly the same height — the slider above changes it for both sides at once. Every bounce is one hit:

  • The first hit uses the starting x, so x! deals 0! = 1 damage; after every hit x grows by the step you set.
  • Every layer multiplies its HP, and a brick only disappears once its HP is below 0 — then the ball falls on to the next layer.
  • The first ball to break through every layer and touch the floor wins. The duel stops the moment that happens.
